Life moves quickly, so this is where I pause for a moment. A collection of places I've wandered, things I've experienced, people I've loved, and small details that make an ordinary day feel special.

“There is beauty in the things we almost Forget.”

Some days are made for going somewhere new. Other days are best spent at home, an open window, and absolutely nowhere else to be. I'm learning to make room for both.

This journal is a scrapbook of those moments. Not every story needs a grand adventure. Sometimes the best memories are the quiet ones that find us when we least expect them.
